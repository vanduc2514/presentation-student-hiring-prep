# Idea Board — Student Hiring Preparation in the AI Era

## Presentation

- **Title:** Student Hiring Preparation in the AI Era
- **Subtitle:** What Fresher and Intern hiring actually tests — and how to prepare from year 3 to first offer.
- **Format:** Campus talkshow. Tech Lead presenting, live Q&A at the end.
- **Duration:** 90 minutes including Q&A (target ~65 min content, ~25 min Q&A)
- **Audience:** Year 3–4 IT students and recent graduates who can already code and already have a CV
- **Language:** Vietnamese primary (`slides/presentation.vi.md`), English mirror (`slides/presentation.en.md`)

## Thesis

Coding ability and a clean CV are **table stakes**, not differentiators. The hiring bar moved: entry-level postings are scarce, applicant volume tripled, and AI made claims cheap. What separates candidates now is **verifiable evidence of judgment** — the ability to decompose a problem, explain a trade-off, and verify work you did not write yourself.

## One-line promise to the audience

> By the end, you will know exactly which of the eight things panels score you are currently failing, and what to do in the next 12 weeks about it.

## Evidence stack (the actionable core)

Five artifacts, each proving something different:

1. **CV** — proves you can summarize yourself for a 7-second skim
2. **GitHub** — proves the code exists and is yours
3. **One deployed project** — proves you can ship, not just follow tutorials
4. **Portfolio / README with decisions** — proves you can explain trade-offs
5. **Referral or internship** — proves someone trusted you with real work
